# CyberBrain v0.3.1 — Promotion Gate Debt Control

> Historical release record. Current operational behavior is documented in `docs/CURRENT_RUNTIME.md` and canonical specs.

This patch release tightens the Dream promotion gate to control review-debt inflow
after the measured 3412-case pending backlog (2026-10-10). No tool contract, schema,
or collection change.

Key changes:

- **Promotion gate:**
  - `minimum_evidence_count` raised from 1 to 2; single-evidence candidates now
    REJECT with `insufficient_evidence_count` instead of entering human review.
  - Paraphrase near-duplicates merge like exact duplicates: token-set Jaccard ≥ 0.72
    with matching entity type, classification, context, polarity, and evidence
    partitions (`evidence_merged_from_near_duplicate`).
  - Policy remains overridable via `PromotionPolicy`; thresholds are deployment
    policy only and do not alter canonical storage or evidence semantics.

- **Version Bump:**
  - Version authority bumped to `0.3.1` in `cyberbrain/_version.py`.
