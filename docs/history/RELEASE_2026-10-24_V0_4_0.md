# CyberBrain v0.4.0 — Living Cognition & Runtime Activation

> Historical release record. Current operational behavior is documented in `docs/CURRENT_RUNTIME.md` and canonical specs.

This minor release activates dormant cognitive subsystems (Knowledge Graph, Prospective Prediction Learning, Concept Formation, and Recovery Hardening), converting static foundation modules into an active, verified, fail-closed runtime loop.

Key changes:

- **Knowledge Graph & Relation Indexing:**
  - Automated in-flight relation linking in Dreaming writeback (`writeback.py`), searching within tenant boundaries (`_partition_conditions`) and creating `RelationKind.SUPPORTS` edges.
  - Offline / maintenance Knowledge Graph autolinker (`scripts/autolink_knowledge_graph.py`) with embedding fallback and strict tenant partition enforcement.

- **Prospective Prediction & Metacognition Calibration (M6):**
  - Production-ready prospective event producer seam (`cyberbrain/cognition/producer.py`) providing `track_prediction` context manager and CLI wrapper with timeout guards and `IdentityTrust.UNSPECIFIED` default.
  - Multi-session, multi-topic calibration verification runner (`scripts/seed_calibration_data.py`) establishing genuine empirical prospective evidence.

- **Concept Formation & Promotion (M4):**
  - Integrated `ConceptPromotionService` and `ConceptPromotionGate` (`cyberbrain/concepts/promotion.py`) to evaluate and store abstracted concept candidates into canonical Knowledge.

- **Dream Writeback & Queue Hardening:**
  - Robust domain fallback in `writeback.py`: defaults to `CYBERBRAIN_DREAM_DEFAULT_DOMAIN` (`engineering`) when evidence is episodic-only, preventing metadata drop.
  - Tenancy-scoped dead-letter queue recovery (`DreamQueue.requeue_all_failed`) with authority snapshot filtering.
  - Clock skew tolerance extended to +3600s in traversal and task inbox validations.

- **Recovery & Operations (F0):**
  - Fail-closed operational backup bundle creator (`scripts/create_f0_recovery_bundle.py`) guaranteeing coherent snapshots of Qdrant collections and operational SQLite databases with SHA-256 verification.

- **Version Bump:**
  - Version authority bumped to `0.4.0` in `cyberbrain/_version.py`.
